How the numbers are made

The costing cycle, from source system to funding decision

Eight steps run every costing cycle. Each one has an owner at QEH, a control that must pass, and an output that the next step depends on. Gold steps are decision gates where QEH signs off before figures are used.

Where the data comes from

Attention to detail

Every figure carries its data-quality grade

Discovery grades each source A–D on four tests. Grades are shown on every output, so decision-makers know how far to rely on a number, and the remediation plan targets the weakest cells first.

Data-readiness by priority service

A: reliable at patient level. B: reliable at service level. C: usable with adjustment. D: not yet usable; interim estimate applied.
